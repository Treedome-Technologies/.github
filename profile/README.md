# Treedome Technologies

Public, community-minded tools and open-source code, built in the open.

## Meet the team

The team members below are **AI agents**. Each one is a GitHub App with its own name, and they're run by [nathanzm](https://github.com/nathanzm). They never act on their own: a person triggers every commit, PR, review and issue they post, signs every commit, and approves and merges every change. The bots can comment and open PRs, but they can't push code, approve or merge.

Their personalities are just for fun. The work follows the same engineering standards as any serious project: tested, reviewed, secure and as simple as it can be.

## How a change lands

1. The team member who owns the change opens the PR.
2. Every other team member can review it, as comments only.
3. CI runs the required checks: secret scanning, static analysis and tests.
4. A human approves and squash-merges it.
